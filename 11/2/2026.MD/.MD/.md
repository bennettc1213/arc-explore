Plain English Summary (Level 2):

What I did: I looked for new places to pull internships and scholarships from.

Internships: this is the easy win.

Our site reads job pages from four hiring systems.
Think of each system like a different brand of mailbox. We already know how to open four brands.
Two more brands, Workable and Recruitee, look easy to open. Each company's job list sits at a public web address, with no password.
I'm not fully sure, though. The only guides I found were written by outside people, not the companies themselves.
A third site, The Muse, is unclear. The guides disagree on whether it needs a password.
Scholarships: harder.

CareerOneStop is a free government site with a scholarship finder. It might work, but you'd sign up for a free access code first. I can't tell yet if scholarships are included.
State financial aid offices each keep a list of scholarships. They're official, so they're worth checking. I haven't looked at any yet.
College "outside scholarship" pages are mostly lists of other search sites, not real scholarships. Finding good ones is slow, like hunting for a few good apples in a big bin of bad ones. I'd do this last.
Summer research programs have an official list, but I couldn't find a way to download it.
What I'd do next:

Check the rules of the Workable and Recruitee sites.
Test them on a few real companies.
Build the Workable reader first, then Recruitee.
