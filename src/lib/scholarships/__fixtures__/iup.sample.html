BOC Sciences Chemistry Scholarship

BOC Sciences is proud to have established a scholarship program that awards $1,000 once a year to college students who are pursuing a chemistry-related field of study. This scholarship aims to support and empower the next generation of chemists, helping unlock their full potential and make significant contributions to the scientific community. Please visit BOC Chemistry Scholarship Program.

Pittsburgh Metropolitan Area Hispanic Chamber of Commerce Foundation (PMAHCC)

The PMAHCC Foundation Scholarship Program accepts applications from January 1 to April 30 for the upcoming scholastic year starting in the fall. Grant amounts are evaluated based on individual need or merit and available trust funds.

Working Student Scholarship

The Law Offices of Mallon Snyder understands the immense sacrifices college students make to pay for their education. We have created the Working Student Scholarship to support those who are partially or fully funding their education. In order to qualify for this $1,000 award, the applicant must be:

  • Partially or fully paying for their own college education;
  • Enrolled or planning to enroll in an undergraduate program within the United States for the Fall 2026 semester;
  • At least 17 years of age;
  • A resident or permanent citizen of the United States.

The deadline for the fall 2026 applications is August 5, 2026.

Additional guidelines can be found at Working Student Scholarship.
