how the limits work
Each Free tool renews 30 days after you first use it, on its own clock. Reopening, editing, copying or exporting what you already made never counts, and a run that fails gives the use back.
The tracker limit counts active opportunities only. Finished ones (offer, rejected, ghosted, withdrawn) and your whole history are always kept and never count. Applications you actually submit are always recorded.
If you leave Pro, nothing is deleted: your drafts, tracker and saved searches stay, and you choose which saved search keeps alerting.
Alerts can only report what we have already found and checked at the source; daily is the fastest we run them.
secure payments via Stripe

Payments are handled by Stripe; card details never reach Instela. Pro is monthly and you can manage or cancel it from this page at any time.
